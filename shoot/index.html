      Location options

      Five spots I think could work. Tell me which ones you like and I will sort out the route. All five are saved together in this Google Maps list if you want to look around them yourself.

      01

      Centennial St & Figueroa Terrace

      Victor Heights · 34.066736, -118.245915

      Residential street up on a hill, and downtown lines up right down the middle of it. Cars parked both sides. The second photo is what we would be going for, sky still blue with the buildings already lit.

      Timing. This only works in about a 25 minute window right after sunset. If you want it, we start here.

      02

      Chinatown Central Plaza

      943 N Broadway · 34.065226, -118.237499

      Lanterns hang the whole length of the plaza and there is neon on the gate and the storefronts. It is all pedestrian, so we are not working around cars.

      The red is already there before I turn anything on, so my light adds to it instead of doing all the work. The walkways are narrow, which suits the fisheye, and the lanterns overhead work for the look-up shot.

      03

      303 S Hewitt St & Traction Ave

      Arts District · 34.045219, -118.236236

      The murals wrap the corner at Traction, floor to roof, both sides of the street. These photos are daytime so you can actually see the art.

      At night the street lights hit the paint and everything around it goes black. I would light you against that. The alley off Traction is where I would shoot the alleyway reels.

      04

      Lower Grand Ave

      Between 3rd and 4th · 34.051368, -118.252126

      The sunken road under downtown, with concrete columns and strip lights running the whole ceiling. Car people shoot here all the time, and it has been in Terminator, Dark Knight Rises, and a stack of Audi and Hyundai commercials.

      It is lit and covered, so it does not matter how late we get there.

      Shot ideas. Two things I would do here. Reel 06, rotating the gimbal up into those ceiling columns so the spin has an actual pattern to turn against. And the falling shot, coming out of one of the tunnel openings under the bridge.

      05

      Urban Light, LACMA

      5905 Wilshire Blvd · 34.063024, -118.359237

      The lamp posts. Free, outside, lit all night. The symmetry does most of the work, so a silhouette or you walking through the middle needs almost no setup.

      The drive. 23 minutes from Lower Grand, 28 from Hewitt. There are usually other people shooting there at night.
